How it works

Gains come from Vladar's “Training Gains Explained” v2.0 formula. Three routines are compared on the same 24-hour drug clock (Xanax ≈ 7 h, ecstasy ≈ 3.6 h of cooldown):

  • Standard train: Xanax on cooldown, one points refill, natural regen, all at your normal happy.
  • Booster jump: a day's boosters, then your full bar, refill and one Xanax at boosted happy. Uses no drug cooldown, so it never loses to a standard train.
  • Ecstasy jump: stack 1–4 Xanax, eat boosters, take ecstasy, train the stack and a refill at doubled happy. Stacking freezes your energy regen and the ecstasy uses drug cooldown, so it only wins while doubled happy is worth more than that lost energy. The calculator tries every stack size and how many ordinary Xanax to take between jumps, and keeps the best.

“Works” means the ecstasy jump makes more stats per day than a booster jump with the same boosters. Overdoses lose the stack (and the boosters, for an ecstasy overdose) and stretch a Xanax cooldown to 24–25 h; happy is assumed to be restored at rehab. Prices are the cheapest item-market listings for the quantity a jump needs.
