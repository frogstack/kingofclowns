When to stop jumping

A first-principles model of three training routines — standard train, booster jump and ecstasy jump — for a donator with a maxed Private Island (5,025 happy). Overdoses are counted throughout. It finds the stat level where the ecstasy stops paying for itself, for every tier of candy and for eDVDs.

Terms

Three routines

These names aren't standard in the community, so here is exactly what each one means in this report. Each one is a full day of training: Xanax, a points refill and natural regen, trained at different happy.

Strategy 1Standard train

No boosters, no ecstasy. Take a Xanax every time the drug cooldown clears (3.43 a day on average), use one points refill, and train all of it plus natural regen at your normal happy.

Strategy 2Booster jump

Eat a day's boosters (up to 48 candies or 4 eDVDs), then train your full bar, a refill and one Xanax at the boosted happy. No ecstasy and no stacking, so it costs no drug cooldown and no regen. The rest of the day's Xanax and regen is trained at normal happy.

Strategy 3Ecstasy jump

Stack 1–4 Xanax above max energy, eat boosters, take an ecstasy to double your happy, then train the whole stack and a refill inside one 15-minute window. Stacking freezes regen and the ecstasy uses 3.6 h of drug cooldown, which is the price of the doubled happy.

What players call a “happy jump” (eDVDs) or a “choco jump” (chocolates) are both ecstasy jumps here. They differ only in the booster, and the booster is a setting in every chart below rather than a separate routine.

Assumptions

The setup

  • Player: donator (150 max energy, 5 energy every 10 minutes = 720/day), maxed Private Island at 5,025 happy, no perks. Perks and gym dots multiply every strategy equally, so they cancel out of every breakeven.
  • Gym: Deep Burn (6.0 dots, 10 E per train). The sensitivity section repeats the ceilings for gyms from Global Gym to Gym 3000; they move by about 1% at most.
  • The 24-hour drug clock. Xanax and ecstasy share one cooldown. Xanax averages 7 h (360–480 min), ecstasy 3.6 h (200–231 min). Every strategy keeps that clock busy, so a standard train runs 24 ÷ 7 = 3.43 Xanax a day rather than a flat 3. Holding it to exactly 3 would leave 3 idle hours that an ecstasy could fill for free.
  • Booster clock: 24 h cap. Candy adds 30 min, an eDVD 6 h. You may use an item while the timer is just under the cap, so one jump can hold up to 49 candies or 5 eDVDs. Over time the timer drains one hour per hour, so the long-run budget is 48 candies or 4 eDVDs per day.
  • Happy above max resets on the 15-minute clock, so every jump is eaten, doubled and trained inside one window. Each 10 E train costs 5 happy on average (40–60% of the energy).
  • Stacking costs energy. Natural regeneration stops while you sit above max energy, and a points refill can't be used then either. Energy is capped at 1,000.
  • Overdoses: Xanax 3% per use, ecstasy 5% (an assumption; no sourced rate exists). An overdose loses the energy stacked so far, and the boosters too for an ecstasy overdose. Happy is restored at rehab, which costs next to nothing, so the only lasting cost is the lost jump.
  • Boosters: the cheapest item in each happy tier at today's market value. One booster type per jump, no mixed stacks.
  • Rates, not totals. Each curve is the stats per day you'd gain running that routine while your stat sits at the value on the x-axis. Where two curves cross is a breakeven.
Showing the work

Derivation

1 · The gym formula

The model uses Vladar's “Training Gains Explained” v2.0, the fit the community's jump calculators use. For one train costing E energy at happy H with stat S:

gain = [ S × round4(1 + 0.07 × round4(ln(1 + H/250))) + 8 × H^1.05 + (1 − (H/99,999)²) × A + B ] × G × E / 200,000 A, B per stat: STR 1600, 1700 SPD 1600, 2000 DEX 1800, 1500 DEF 2100, −600 G = gym dots (6.0 at Deep Burn). S is capped at 50M, H at 99,999 inside the formula.

Read it as two pieces. The stat term grows with your stat but sees happy only through a logarithm. The happy term grows almost linearly with happy but doesn't care about your stat. At low stats the happy term dominates, so doubling happy nearly doubles your gains. At high stats the stat term dominates, and doubling happy adds only a few percent.

As a cross-check I reran every ceiling with the older single formula on the Torn wiki's Gym page, fitted independently. The two agree within about 5% on every ceiling.

2 · Why a ceiling has to exist

The chart shows how much more each energy is worth at jump happy than at 5,025, at every stat level. An ecstasy jump trains fewer energy per day (stacking freezes your regen and the ecstasy uses drug cooldown), so it only wins while that multiplier beats the energy you gave up.

Gain per energy at jump happy ÷ gain per energy at 5,025, strength. Dashed line at 1.76: a 3-Xanax mint jump trains 984 E a day against a standard train's 1,727, so it needs a 1.76× multiplier just to tie. Where a curve crosses the line is roughly where that jump loses to a standard train before overdoses; happy decay and the extra Xanax happy move the exact point a little.

5 · Breakeven definitions

  • Ceiling (vs booster jump): the ecstasy jump makes fewer stats per day than a booster jump with the same boosters. This is the point to drop the ecstasy.
  • Floor (vs standard train): the ecstasy jump makes fewer stats per day than plain training with no boosters at all. Past this you'd be paying for boosters to go backwards.
  • Stack sizes 1–4 are each modelled at their best cadence: either back-to-back, or with ordinary Xanax trained between jumps when that lets you jump less often and use the full booster budget.
Strategy 2

Booster jumps never lose

A booster jump costs no drug cooldown and no regen, so it is a strict improvement on a standard train at every stat level. Its edge shrinks in percentage terms as your stats grow, because the stat term swamps the happy term.

One expectation didn't hold up: booster jumps get cheaper per extra stat as you grow, not dearer. The extra gains in absolute stats keep rising with your stat while the booster bill stays fixed. The dollars section has the numbers.

Putting it together

Race to 400k strength

Seven ways to take strength from 10,000 to 400,000. Every run trains at Deep Burn the whole way, puts all its energy into strength, counts overdoses, and uses each cooldown the moment it ends. Days come from adding up 1 ÷ (stats per day) across every stat level on the way; spend adds up the Xanax, boosters, ecstasy and refills bought over those days.

Strength against days, starting from 10,000. Dashed line: standard trains all the way.

Strategies 2–4 stack 3 Xanax every jump whatever the stat. Strategies 5 and 6 pick the best stack size at each stat and switch at the ceilings. The same plan with cupcakes reaches 400k in 21.1 days but costs $3.9B. “$ per day saved” is the extra spend over strategy 1 divided by the days saved.

Strategy 6, step by step

What the race shows

  • Stacking cheap candy forever is the slowest option. 3-Xanax mint jumps all the way to 400k take 42 days, 27% longer than never jumping. Past about 31k a 3-Xanax mint stack makes fewer stats than a standard train.
  • Stopping at the ceiling is what counts. Mints, then truffles, then standard trains (strategy 5) saves 2.4 days for $30M extra. Full min-max (strategy 6) saves 3.5 days but costs nearly three times as much as standard trains, and $103M of that goes on half a day of Birthday Cupcakes.
  • The minimum booster is the cheapest way to make ecstasy pay, not the fastest way to grow. Short of spending about $200M a day on Birthday Cupcakes, eDVDs are the fastest booster at every stat level. eDVD ecstasy jumps to 211k, then eDVD booster jumps, reach 400k in 22 days for $424M. Stacking 3 Xanax with eDVDs all the way is 1.5 days slower and $25M dearer.
Risk

Overdoses

An overdose wipes energy and happy. For a stacked jump that's the whole stack, and an ecstasy overdose also throws away the boosters you just ate. I couldn't find a sourced per-use rate for ecstasy. Hank's training guide on TornStats treats Xanax as 3% per use, and the Torn wiki only says ecstasy's chance is relatively high. So every headline number assumes Xanax 3% and ecstasy 5%, and the table below shows how the ceiling moves as the ecstasy rate changes.

What's counted: the energy lost, the drug used, the boosters lost on an ecstasy overdose, and the longer 24–25 h cooldown after a Xanax overdose. Hospital time is assumed to be revived, price not counted. Happy is restored at rehab for next to nothing, so it costs nothing lasting.

Robustness

Sensitivity checks

Gym doesn't matter

Gym dots multiply every routine by the same factor, so they cancel. The only residue is happy loss being charged in bigger chunks at 50 E-per-train specialist gyms.

Stat barely matters

The per-stat constants A and B only shift the happy-independent part of the formula. Defense runs about 1,500 higher than the rest, which is under 5% at every tier. Overdoses counted in every table below.

A 48 h booster cap does not double the ceiling

The booster timer drains one hour per hour whatever the cap, so a bigger cap never raises your daily booster budget. It only lets you save two days of boosters for one bigger jump, which means jumping half as often. For 100-happy candy and up that trade is worth about 1.4–1.5× on the ceiling; for cheap candy it's worth little or nothing, because the cheapest jumps are already limited by how often you jump, not by the cap.
